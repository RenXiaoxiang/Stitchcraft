# Coffee roastery — raw request

I run a small specialty coffee roastery in Chengdu called **Ember & Bean**. We
roast single-origin beans in small batches and sell two ways: a monthly
subscription delivered nationwide, and wholesale beans to cafés.

Right now we only have a WeChat channel and a Linktree. It looks amateur and
café owners cannot find our wholesale terms. I want a proper website.

## What the site needs to do

1. Make a first-time visitor understand our story and why our beans are
   different (freshly roasted, traceable farms, small batches).
2. Convert visitors into subscription sign-ups — the form can link out to our
   existing store for now, no full checkout in phase one.
3. Give cafés a wholesale page with our terms, minimum order, and an inquiry
   form.
4. Show the current bean lineup with tasting notes.

## Brand notes

- Warm, craft, a little editorial — not a generic SaaS template.
- Chinese first, with an English version of the "About" and "Wholesale" pages
  for visiting buyers.
- We have a logo (roast flame mark) and about 30 photos from the roastery;
  most are phone photos, usable but inconsistent.

## Constraints

- Static hosting, must load fast on mobile.
- Budget is tight: keep it to one hero image per page, no fancy animation.
- We want to update the bean lineup ourselves after launch without calling a
  developer every month.

## Timeline

Soft launch in three weeks, wholesale page can follow a week later.
