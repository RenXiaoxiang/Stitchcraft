Why we roast

We started with one question: what if coffee tasted like the place it came from?

Hollow Roast is a two-person roastery in a former print shop on SE Division. We buy small lots, roast them light enough to keep the fruit, and rest them until they taste like themselves.

The Ember Set is our autumn release: a washed Ethiopian, a honey-processed Colombian, and a natural Brazilian. Three farms we have visited, three profiles we have argued about, one box.

No subscriptions to cancel. No tasting notes written to impress a score sheet. Just a roast that works in a flat white and is interesting enough to drink black on a Tuesday.

“If the coffee is good, the ceremony is optional.”

— Mara & Ilya, founders

The release

From green bean to your counter.

We roast once, pack the same day and ship within the week. The timeline below is the whole operation — no warehouse, no middle step.

  1. 20 Sep

    Our last sample roast

    Mara profiles the Ethiopian lot for the fourth time. Ilya votes for more rest.

  2. 04 Oct

    Pre-orders open

    240 bags of the Ember Set, first come. We close the list when it is full.

  3. 18 Oct

    Roast day

    All three profiles go on the drum in one long, loud morning.

  4. 25 Oct

    Bags leave the shop

    Packed with the roast date and a card from the farm. Then we clean the drum.
